The AI, explained

The examiner

An exact optimizer tries every possible order of the deliveries, so it always finds the best one. But the number of orders explodes: 7 requests have 5,040 orders, 10 have 3.6 million, 15 have 1.3 trillion. It is far too slow for a real factory.

The AI

A machine-learning ranker learned from thousands of the examiner's answers. It decides in about 40 ms, however many requests wait, and picks the same first delivery about 9 times in 10.

This page

A real factory would run only the AI. Here the examiner also checks every decision live, to prove the AI is almost as good as perfect.
