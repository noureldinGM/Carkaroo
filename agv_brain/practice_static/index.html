Simulator Practice

Practise connecting your factory

This is not the Carkaroo website. It pretends to be your car and your stations, and logs in to the website's broker with their keys, exactly like the real ones will. Keep the Carkaroo website open next to it (http://localhost:8050) and watch each device turn green there.

1 The factory floor

Which factory to pretend: its track and its stations, as you built them on the Carkaroo website.

2 Connect

On the Carkaroo website: Setup → Devices → Copy all for the simulator. Paste it here. Or type each key below, like you will in the firmware.

3 The pretend devices

Each one logs in with its own ID and key. A wrong key is refused, like on the real car. Disconnect is like pulling its plug.
