Roo's moods

Roo reacts to what really happens in the factory. The website reads the live data three times a second and picks one mood with the rules below. The logo's eyes and Roo on the Now page show the same mood, and Roo says one short line about it. Try the moods here, move your mouse around (Roo looks at you when it's calm), and switch to dark mode.

When Roo shows each mood

From top to bottom, the first rule that matches wins. A mood stays at least 2.5 seconds, so Roo doesn't flicker. "Curious" is the one exception: it shows for 3.5 seconds after you change something in Try it on the Now page.
